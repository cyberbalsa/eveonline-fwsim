This material is used with limited permission of CCP Games. No official affiliation or endorsement by CCP Games is stated or implied.

See the [EVE Online Content Creation Terms of Use](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/8563917741084-EVE-Online-Content-Creation-Terms-of-Use). The official-image files are preserved without alteration; ordinary proportional display scaling does not change their stored bytes. The [identity asset manifest](assets/identities/provenance.json) gives URLs, dates, file hashes, and identity IDs. These assets are not licensed as newly authored project source code.

Real in-game names identify documented entities. Simulated decisions are authored campaign behavior, not real quotations, endorsements, private biographies, or a claim about the individuals behind the characters. Current official images are not evidence of historical appearance. Community artwork outside the official service is not included without its own permission/provenance.

Public geography and occupancy data come from ESI and CCP's SDE. The geographic files were inherited from `../eveonline-risk`, preserving their original provenance. The fresh FW observation, copied raw files, and verification limits are documented in the [research manifest](research/raw/manifest.json).

Freeciv and Freeciv-web are credited as inspected open-source references. Their repositories contain GPL/AGPL-covered code with their respective notices. No Freeciv source has been incorporated into production game code here. Any future source adaptation must preserve attribution and comply with the license of the actual files used. [Source audit and license discussion](research/06-freeciv-evaluation.md).

Civilization is referenced as a game-design inspiration; no Civilization code or artwork is included. The formal research and participant sources are credited in the [source index](research/SOURCES.md).

The playable interface also uses historical CCP icons, actual ship geometry, a converted EVE client nebula cubemap and prepared EVE audio cues. [Detailed art and renderer provenance](ASSETS.md) documents source hashes, transformations and visual limitations. Third-party Three.js and Barlow fonts retain their MIT and SIL Open Font License notices respectively. The identity and official-render bytes are unchanged; converted geometry/backgrounds and project materials are described separately.
